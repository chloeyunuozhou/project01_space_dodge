SPACE DODGE V2

Salamander-style shooter · Dodge comets, fight mini bees, defeat five bosses and win.

About the Game

Space Dodge V2 is inspired by Konami's classic Salamander (1986, aka Life Force)—a vertical scrolling shooter. Pilot your ship at the bottom of the screen. Dodge falling comets (yellow = 1 life, purple = 2 lives, blue = 3 lives) and enemy bullets from small bees. When no boss is on screen, mini bees appear—shoot them to clear the way. Collect heart bubbles (every 15 seconds) to gain extra lives; you start with 3 lives and can hold up to 9. Collect power-up capsules: Speed (faster movement), Spread (3-way shot), Shield (temporary invincibility), Laser (continuous beam), Shotgun (fan of pellets), and Missile (homing shots for 8 seconds). Five bosses appear over time: Big Bee, Shark, Pufferfish, Robot, and Gorilla. Defeat the final boss (Gorilla) to win. Background music plays when you start; use the Music button to mute.

How to Play

  • Move your spacecraft left and right with the arrow keys.
  • Shoot with SPACE to destroy mini bees and damage bosses.
  • Dodge comets and enemy bullets—each hit costs lives (or 3 for blue comets).
  • Collect heart bubbles to gain lives (up to 9) and capsules for weapons and power-ups.
  • Defeat all five bosses in order; beating the Gorilla wins the game.
